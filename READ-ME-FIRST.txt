MERCATO STORE - how to run (Windows)

You need two things installed once: Node.js (nodejs.org, LTS) and MySQL (you already have it).

FIRST TIME
1. Double-click START.bat
2. Notepad opens twice. Fill these and save:
   Backend  (first Notepad):  DB_PASS = your MySQL password,  STRIPE_SECRET_KEY = your sk_test_... key
   Frontend (second Notepad): VITE_STRIPE_PUBLISHABLE_KEY = your pk_test_... key
3. Wait while it installs. Two black windows open and the website opens in Chrome.
   Do not close the black windows.
4. On the website, click Log in, then Create an account, and register.
5. Double-click MAKE-ADMIN.bat and type the same email. Then log out and log in on the website.
6. Double-click ADD-PRODUCTS.bat to fill the store with 660 products.

EVERY NEXT TIME
Just double-click START.bat. Close the two black windows to stop the store.

FOLDERS
backend   = the server (Node + Express + MySQL)
frontend  = the website (React)
Do not move files out of these two folders.
